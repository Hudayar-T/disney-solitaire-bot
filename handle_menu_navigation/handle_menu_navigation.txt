1. main_menu.jpg
This is how the main menu of the game looks like.
If there is a "Collect" purple button on the center bottom, that is an hourly bonus ready. It has to be clicked and collected before diving into playing.
When nothing is hindering, the green triangle button on the right bottom - the play button - should be pressed.

2. one_more_play_button.jpg
This is the window that pops up after clicking the play button on the main menu. The green "PLAY" button should be clicked here to proceed.

3. random_reward.jpg
This is a kind of window that randomly keeps popping up for completing various missions subconsciously. Just tapping anywhere solves the issue.
